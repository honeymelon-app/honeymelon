## Recommended Strategy

### Pricing Model: **FREEMIUM**

#### Free Tier

- ✅ Basic conversions (H.264, AAC, MP3)
- ✅ 5 conversions per day
- ✅ Single file processing
- ✅ Standard quality only
- ❌ No hardware acceleration
- ❌ No batch processing
- ❌ No advanced codecs

#### Pro Tier: **$19.99 (one-time)**

- ✅ Unlimited conversions
- ✅ All codecs (HEVC, VP9, AV1, ProRes)
- ✅ Batch processing
- ✅ All quality tiers
- ✅ Hardware acceleration
- ✅ Priority support
- ✅ Lifetime updates (same major version)

### Why Freemium?

1. **Compete with free alternatives** — Users can try it first
2. **Lower barrier to entry** — Build user base through word-of-mouth
3. **Clear upgrade path** — Free users become Pro advocates
4. **Sustainable business** — 10% conversion rate = viable business
5. **User validation** — Learn what features people actually pay for

### Why NOT $49.99+?

You don't have:

- ❌ Advanced features (AI, advanced editing, automation)
- ❌ Brand recognition or market validation
- ❌ Significant advantages over free alternatives
- ❌ Proven customer satisfaction

**You can get there in 6-12 months** with the right features.

## My Final Recommendation

### Should You Sell This? **YES, ABSOLUTELY.**

You've built something valuable. Don't give it away for free.

**Start with freemium at $19.99 Pro tier.**

- Competes with free alternatives by offering free tier
- Pro tier is affordable impulse-buy territory

- Can increase later as you add features

### What's the Path to Success?

1. **Launch smart** — Freemium model, good marketing
2. **Listen to users** — They'll tell you what to build next
3. **Iterate quickly** — Ship features based on feedback
4. **Focus on UX** — Your advantage over HandBrake
5. **Build premium features** — AI, automation, advanced editing
6. **Grow the price** — $19.99 → $29.99 → $39.99 → $49.99

## Questions to Consider

Before launching, ask yourself:

1. **Are you willing to support paying customers?**
   - Customer support is time-consuming
   - Paid users have high expectations

2. **Can you commit to ongoing development?**
   - One-time payment = lifetime updates expectation
   - Need to keep adding features

3. **Do you have time for marketing?**
   - Great software doesn't sell itself
   - Need consistent marketing effort

4. **Are you prepared for competition?**
   - HandBrake might add remux-first
   - Permute might add similar features

5. **What's your exit strategy?**
   - Build to $1M/year and run it?
   - Build to sell to larger company?

**Think through these before committing.**
